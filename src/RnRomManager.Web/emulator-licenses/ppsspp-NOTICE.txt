PPSSPP (PlayStation Portable) for EmulatorJS 4.2.3
==================================================

Files: ppsspp-thread-wasm.data, ppsspp-assets.zip and reports/ppsspp.json from the
npm package @emulatorjs/core-ppsspp 4.2.3, copied unchanged. The package has no
non-threaded or legacy (WebGL 1) payload: its report sets defaultWebGL2 and
requireThreads, so RnRomManager plays PSP only in a cross-origin isolated page.

Source
------
Core: https://github.com/EmulatorJS/ppsspp, a fork that tracks
hrydgard/ppsspp. The payload's report records a build started at
2025-06-14T19:28:55Z; the fork's master branch was then at
47efdd01da182e2a8f8f2ed078d56fd230480432. The payload's license.txt is
byte-identical to LICENSE.TXT at that commit. Build: core.json runs
libretro/emscripten/build_emscripten.sh and RetroArch's
"build-emulatorjs.sh --clean --threads" (see COMMON-BUILD.txt for the
RetroArch fork, EmulatorJS build scripts and Emscripten version).

ppsspp-assets.zip
-----------------
166 entries (about 19.6 MB unpacked), loaded into the emulator's virtual
filesystem at /PPSSPP. Traced against assets/ at commit 47efdd0 by git blob
hash: 87 files are byte-identical, including every flash0/font/*.pgf file,
the VFPU tables, sounds and images. 54 text and data files (compat.ini,
compatvr.ini, knownfuncs.ini, langregion.ini, gamecontrollerdb.txt, lang/*.ini,
shaders/defaultshaders.ini, shaders/tex_mmpx.csh, themes/defaultthemes.ini,
the ui_atlas files and Roboto-Condensed.ttf) differ from that commit's copies
and come from a nearby revision of the same tree. Unless noted below they are
PPSSPP's own data under its GPL-2.0-or-later licence.

- debugger/: the ppsspp-debugger web build (submodule
  https://github.com/unknownbrackets/ppsspp-debugger at
  9776332f720c854ef26f325a0cf9e32c02115a9c), GPL-3.0-or-later; its bundled
  JavaScript dependencies list their licences in
  debugger/static/js/main.fe87e942.js.LICENSE.txt inside the archive.
- gamecontrollerdb.txt: SDL_GameControllerDB (zlib licence).
- Roboto-Condensed.ttf: Google's Roboto Condensed (Apache-2.0).
- flash0/font/*.pgf: PSP system fonts as shipped in PPSSPP's assets/
  (embedded names "FTT-NewRodin Pro" and "AsiaKNHH-SONY-uni"). These are
  Sony PSP firmware fonts. No licence for them is published by PPSSPP,
  EmulatorJS or the npm package; RnRomManager redistributes the upstream
  archive unchanged, as PPSSPP and EmulatorJS do (decision recorded in the
  PSP, Genesis and Master System browser play spec).

No PSP firmware or BIOS is shipped or requested; PPSSPP runs games with its
high-level emulation of the PSP operating system.
