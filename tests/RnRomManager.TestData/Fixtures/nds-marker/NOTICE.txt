nds-marker.nds: what it contains and under which terms
=====================================================

nds-marker.nds is built from source/main.c and Makefile in this folder with build.sh, in the
devkitpro/devkitarm image pinned there by digest (the 2024-05-11 image: devkitARM r64, GCC 14.1.0,
libnds 1.8.2, default-arm7 0.7.4, dswifi 0.4.2, maxmod-nds 1.0.15, ndstool 2.3.1). source/main.c, Makefile
and build.sh are original to RnRomManager and MIT-licensed like the rest of the repository.

The cartridge links these devkitPro libraries:

- libnds 1.8.2 on the ARM9 (libnds9.a: system start-up, interrupts, FIFO, keys, video, BIOS calls; from
  the linker map's archive members) and in libnds' default ARM7 binary (default.elf): Copyright (C)
  2005 - 2008 Michael Noland (joat), Jason Rogers (dovoto), Dave Murphy (WinterMute). zlib licence
  (licenses/libnds-LICENSE.txt, the image's /opt/devkitpro/libnds/libnds_license.txt).

- dswifi 0.4.2, linked into default.elf: Copyright (c) 2005-2006 Stephen Stair. MIT licence
  (licenses/dswifi-LICENSE.txt, the image's /opt/devkitpro/libnds/dswifi_license.txt).

- maxmod 1.0.15, linked into default.elf: Copyright (c) 2008, Mukunda Johnson. ISC-style licence
  (licenses/maxmod-LICENSE.txt, the image's /opt/devkitpro/libnds/maxmod_license.txt).

- newlib (on the ARM9, libc.a: init, memcpy-stub; and devkitPro's libsysbase.a: build_argv, malloc_vars;
  default.elf links newlib too), as built for devkitARM. Its licences are collected in
