nds-marker.nds: what it contains and under which terms
=====================================================

nds-marker.nds is built from source/main.c and Makefile in this folder with build.sh, in the
devkitpro/devkitarm image pinned there by digest (devkitARM r68, GCC 16.1.0, libnds 2.0.2, calico 1.2.0,
newlib 4.6.0.20260123, ndstool 2.3.1). source/main.c, Makefile and build.sh are original to RnRomManager
and MIT-licensed like the rest of the repository.

  Jason Rogers (dovoto), Dave Murphy (WinterMute), fincs. zlib licence (licenses/libnds-LICENSE.txt, the
